# Product Marketing Case Study — ReSource

## Overview

ReSource is a guided learning product focused on psychological wellbeing, self-worth, and self-clarity.
This case demonstrates how product marketing was used to structure user understanding in a high-emotional, low-clarity category.
The work spans positioning, messaging design, and go-to-market execution, with a strong focus on how narrative shapes product perception.

## Role

Marketing & Creative Lead

- Owned positioning and messaging
- Designed go-to-market strategy
- Executed full-cycle campaigns
- Built content and narrative systems
- Integrated AI tools into marketing workflows

## Key Focus

- Positioning in a saturated and ambiguous category
- Structuring user understanding through messaging
- Narrative-driven content systems
- Iterative, performance-based GTM execution

## Core Principle

Product understanding is not delivered — it is constructed through messaging.

In this case, messaging acted as a product layer that shaped how users interpret value.
