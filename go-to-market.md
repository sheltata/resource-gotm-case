# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Strategy Overview

GTM was designed as a continuous learning system, not a one-time launch.

The goal was not only acquisition, but gradual construction of user understanding through messaging.

## Channels

- Paid social (primary acquisition)
- Organic content (narrative development)
- Creative testing loops

## Execution Model

- Iterative message validation through content
- Rapid creative iteration cycles
- AI-assisted production for speed and consistency

## Narrative System

Content was designed as a structured progression, not as isolated posts.
Sequential narrative-driven content guided users through:

1. Problem framing  
2. Symptom recognition  
3. Reframing of the problem  
4. Introduction of structured resolution  

This created a multi-step engagement model instead of single-message exposure.

## Key Principle

We do not “communicate value” in one step.
We guide users toward understanding through staged messaging.
