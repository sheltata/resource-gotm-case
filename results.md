# Results & Learnings

## Results

- 4× audience growth
- 7× reach increase
- 3.5× revenue growth on launches
- 6× increase in engagement

## What Worked

- Outcome-driven positioning instead of content-first messaging
- Sequential narrative structure increasing engagement depth
- Fast iteration cycles in messaging and creative

## Key Learnings

1. Messaging clarity directly impacts conversion efficiency  
2. Structured narrative increases engagement more than isolated content  
3. Speed of iteration is more important than single-message optimization  

## Product Insight

In high-emotional categories, messaging is not a layer on top of the product.

Messaging *is part of the product experience*.
